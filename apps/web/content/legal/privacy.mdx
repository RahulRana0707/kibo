## 1. What we collect

Your account details (name, email and sign-in method), the knowledge you add, your bot settings, and the chat messages Kibo reads while it runs in your streams.

## 2. How we use it

To answer and moderate in your chat, build your stream summaries and analytics, keep your account secure, and support you when you ask for help.

## 3. Viewer data

Kibo processes viewer usernames and messages from the platforms you connect, only to answer, moderate and summarise your streams. Shared summaries hide viewer names.

## 4. Sharing

We use a small set of service providers to host and run Kibo. We don’t sell personal data or share it for advertising.

## 5. Retention and deletion

You can export or delete your data from Settings at any time. After you cancel, we keep your data for 30 days so you can come back, then delete it within 60 days.

## 6. Contact

Privacy questions: [privacy@kibo.gg](mailto:privacy@kibo.gg)
