## 1. Look after your community

Don’t set Kibo up to harass, deceive or target viewers, or to post hateful, sexual or violent content.

## 2. Follow platform rules

Kibo works inside YouTube, Twitch and other platforms, so their community guidelines apply to everything your bot says and does.

## 3. No spam or manipulation

Don’t use Kibo to spam other channels, inflate engagement, or impersonate another creator.

## 4. Keep it secure

Don’t try to break into Kibo, other workspaces or the platforms Kibo connects to.

## 5. Reporting

Report misuse to [abuse@kibo.gg](mailto:abuse@kibo.gg).
