Twitch sign-ins expire from time to time, or stop working if you change your Twitch password. When that happens Kibo can’t read Twitch chat, and you’ll see **Needs reconnect** on the Integrations page and a banner at the top of Kibo.

<Callout>Your bots, knowledge and settings are safe. Only the connection needs refreshing.</Callout>

## Reconnect in three steps

1. Open **Integrations** from the sidebar and choose **Twitch**.
2. Select **Reconnect Twitch** and sign in with the same Twitch account.
3. Approve the permissions. The status changes to **Connected** within a few seconds.

## If it disconnects mid-stream

The live console shows a warning and keeps working on your other platforms. Reconnect from your phone without leaving the stream — the console picks Twitch back up where it left off.

## Still not connecting?

Make sure the Twitch account you sign in with is the channel owner, not a moderator account. If you use two-factor on Twitch, have your code ready.
