## The usual suspects

Whatever you stream, most questions fall into five buckets: **what** you’re doing (game, song, recipe), **when** you stream next, **what you use** (gear, settings), **where else** to find you, and **how to join in** (Discord, giveaways, viewer games).

Write one FAQ for each, and Kibo can cover most of the repeat questions in a typical stream on day one.

## Write answers like you talk

Short beats complete. One sentence, one link. If an answer needs a paragraph, it probably needs a command like `!setup` that links to a page.

### Do

“0.32 at 800 DPI, scoped 1.0. Full settings: !setup”

### Don’t

“My sensitivity settings are as follows: in-game sensitivity is set to 0.32 and my mouse DPI is…”

## Keep them fresh

Schedules and sponsors change. Knowledge health flags items you haven’t touched in 60 days, and conflicting answers, so your bot never quotes last month’s promo code.

## Let chat teach you

Everything Kibo can’t answer lands in the Unanswered inbox, grouped by meaning. Spend five minutes after each stream answering the top few and your coverage climbs every week.
