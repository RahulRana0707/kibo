## One stream, two chats

If you simulcast, you now see both chats in a single live feed, each message marked with its platform. Kibo answers each viewer where they asked.

## What carries across

Your knowledge base, personality, moderation rules and commands work the same on both platforms. Ban someone on Twitch and Kibo can flag them on YouTube too.

## Getting started

Open Integrations, choose Connect Twitch and approve the permissions. Your next stream summary covers both platforms.
