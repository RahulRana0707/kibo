## The first minute matters

A new chatter who gets no reply reads it as “this isn’t for me”. A short, personal hello changes that.

## Make the welcome specific

Mention what’s happening right now and one way to join in: “Welcome in! We’re on day 14 of the Immortal grind — !discord for customs.”

## Don’t welcome the same person twice

Kibo remembers who has chatted before, so regulars don’t get greeted like strangers.
