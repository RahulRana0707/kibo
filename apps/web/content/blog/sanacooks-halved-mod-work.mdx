## The problem with long streams

Viewers join at every point of a six-hour stream, and each one asks what’s cooking and where the recipe is.

## The setup

Sana added her recipes as knowledge, set a timer that posts the current recipe link when chat is active, and kept moderation on Normal.

## The result

Her mods now spend their time talking with viewers instead of pasting links.
