## Answer, don’t interrupt

When a viewer asks what drink you’re having, that’s the moment for the promo code. Add your sponsor as knowledge and Kibo mentions it when it’s relevant.

## Keep timers rare

A sponsor timer every 20 minutes, only when chat is active, is plenty. More than that and viewers tune it out.
