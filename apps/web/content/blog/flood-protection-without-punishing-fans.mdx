## Two raids, one spike

Both send hundreds of new chatters in a few seconds. The difference shows in what they say: a friendly raid repeats the raider’s emotes and greets you, a hate raid repeats the same message or slur from brand-new accounts.

## Start gentle

Turn on slow mode automatically during spikes, not followers-only. Slow mode lets raiders say hello and keeps the wall of text readable. Kibo switches it off again once chat settles.

## Escalate on content, not volume

Let the toxicity filter and repeated-message rule decide who gets timed out. Volume alone should never ban anyone.
